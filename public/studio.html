The studio

I'm Zaira Christa, a cultural strategist and creative director in London. I work with independent restaurants, bars and cultural venues that want to be noticed, not necessarily bigger.

Most creative studios begin with a mood board. I begin with your room: who comes, why they come back, and what the place means to them. Cultural research tells me what your venue already is. Design, writing and content then say it clearly, in one voice, from the menu to the website to the first post of the month.

You get the thinking and the finished work from the same person, so nothing is left for you to translate into action. Every package starts with understanding your guests and ends with work you can use the next day.

01 · 6 weeks

Venue Relaunch

Your venue, rebuilt around the people who love it.

A six-week relaunch that begins with research and ends with a new brand direction, a refreshed Instagram, a month of content and printed touches, all made for you.

Phase one: Understand weeks 1 to 2

  1. Discovery Conversation with the owner: what the room is, who the regulars are, what you wish more people understood.
  2. Audience Reading: who actually comes, why they come, and what the place means to them.
  3. Neighbourhood and Competitor Map: who else your guests could choose, and what each of them signals.
  4. The Gap: one page naming what your venue is, what it currently signals, and where those two part ways. You approve it before anything is designed.

This is for you if

  • You run an independent venue that people like but do not talk about enough.
  • You suspect the place is better than it looks, and you want it done, not explained.
  • You would rather decide once, with evidence, and then see the finished work.

Phase two: Make weeks 3 to 6

  1. Brand Direction: voice, colour, type and imagery, written so anyone on your team can follow it.
  2. Instagram Refresh: new profile, grid and highlights, designed and set up for you.
  3. First Month of Content: posts, captions and stories, made and scheduled.
  4. Menu and Print Touches: menu layout, table cards or signage, so the room matches the screen.
  5. Handover Call: what I made, why it works, and how to keep it going.

What you will see, and when

The Gap at the end of week 2; brand direction and Instagram designs mid-way through phase two; the finished content and print files before launch. You approve at each point.

How I make sure you are happy: approval at every stage, two rounds of revisions per deliverable, and terms agreed in writing before we start.

02 · [4 to 6 weeks]

Website Build

A website that sounds like your room.

A finished, mobile-first website built on what I learn about your guests, with every word and every photo direction written for you.

What is included

  1. Short Discovery Conversation and Audience Reading, so the site is built for real guests.
  2. Site Structure: which pages you need and why.
  3. Written Copy for every page, in your venue's voice.
  4. Photography Direction: a shot list your photographer or phone can follow.
  5. Design and Build, launched on your domain.
  6. Booking, menu and contact set up properly.
  7. Handover Call and a short guide to editing it yourself.

What you will see, and when

Site structure and copy first, then the design on desktop and phone, then the live site for a final check before launch. You approve at each point.

This is for you if

  • Your current site is out of date, hard to use on a phone, or does not sound like you.
  • You want guests to understand the place before they arrive.
  • You want one person responsible for the writing, the design and the build.

How I make sure you are happy: you approve the copy before it is designed and the design before it is built, two rounds of revisions per stage, and terms agreed in writing.

Monthly Partner

A creative partner on your side, every month.

Ongoing content, upkeep and review, produced for you and checked against what your guests actually respond to.

What is included each month

  1. Content Plan for the month, tied to your calendar and your regulars.
  2. Posts, captions and stories, made and scheduled.
  3. Website upkeep: menus, hours, events and small changes.

This is for you if

  • You have a brand you like and no time to keep it alive.
  • You would rather have steady, thoughtful output than a burst of activity every few months.

How I make sure you are happy: you see the plan before the month begins, you approve what goes out, and the monthly review shows you what it did. [Set your notice period, for example one month.]

How it works

  1. I listen. A conversation about the room, the regulars and what you wish more people understood.
  2. I read. Your guests, your neighbourhood and your neighbours, studied closely.
  3. I name the gap. One page on what your venue is, what it currently signals, and where those two part ways.
  4. I make it. Brand, content, website, print. Finished, not sketched.
  5. I stay, if you want me to. A monthly partnership that keeps the work alive.

How I make sure you are happy with it

  • You approve every stage before I move to the next, so nothing arrives as a surprise.
  • You see the thinking before the design: the Gap comes first, and everything after is checked against it.
  • Revisions are built into every package (two rounds per deliverable).
  • Terms are fixed in writing before any work begins.
  • You deal with me directly, from first call to handover.

FAQ

Do you only work with restaurants?
My first focus is independent hospitality: restaurants, bars, cafes and cultural venues. I also take on a small number of fashion and creative brands. If you are somewhere in between, ask.
Why does research come first?
Because guessing is expensive. Two weeks of understanding your guests saves months of making things that look good and do nothing.
Will I need to do anything with the strategy?
No. I build the finished work. You review and approve it, and you get a short guide to keeping it going.
What if I do not like what you make?
You approve every stage before the next begins, and revisions are included (two rounds per deliverable). The Gap comes first precisely so we agree on the direction before any design exists.
Do you use AI?
Yes, for research, drafts and production. I make every decision about taste and direction, and I check every piece before it reaches you.
I run a small venue. Is this for me?
It is designed for you. I work with independent places that want to be more noticed, not with chains.
How do I begin?
Tell me about your venue using the form. If it looks like a fit, I will arrange a short call, free of charge, and recommend a first step.

Let me look at your room properly.

Tell me about your venue and I will tell you, honestly, where I think the gap is.
