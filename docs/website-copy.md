## About page

**Headline**

I think before I design.

**Opening**

I'm Zaira Christa, a cultural strategist and creative director in London. Agencies look confident and rarely explain why their work should work. Academics explain culture beautifully and rarely make anything a restaurant can use on Friday night. I work between the two: cultural research first, finished work after, both by me.

**Background**

- **Study.** BA Music, University of Oxford. MA Cultural and Creative Industries, King's College London. My research asks how places, clothes and sound carry meaning, and how that meaning becomes value.
- **Practice.** Brand strategy and social media for four independent hospitality brands in West London \[confirm which venue names you will publish\].
- **Making.** A fashion label shown in two runway shows. Levi's. Co-director of the Oxford Fashion Gala.

**Belief**

- A venue is a cultural space. Guests come for the food and stay for what the place says about them.
- Evidence sharpens taste. The best decisions are the ones you can explain.
- Independent does not mean small-minded. Be more noticed without becoming a chain.
- Advice you cannot use is not worth buying. I make the thing, not only the plan.

**How I work**

You work with me directly, with no account manager in between. AI carries the heavy research and first drafts. I make every decision about taste and direction, and I check every piece before it reaches you.

If you run a room people love, I would like to hear about it.
